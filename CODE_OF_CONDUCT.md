# Community conduct

Be respectful. Discuss the work, welcome questions, and give useful feedback.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, and sharing private
information without consent are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately using the contact details on
[envoy1084's profile](https://github.com/envoy1084). Ask for a private channel
if needed; do not post details about the people involved.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. Reports will
be shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

This applies to EffectKit issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
